📲 Install ReelVault on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆️ at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch ReelVault from your Home Screen for a full-screen native experience!
💡 Saving Videos to Camera Roll: When you tap "Save to iPhone" on any video, iOS opens the Share sheet—tap "Save Video" to save straight to your Photos app, or "Save to Files"!
